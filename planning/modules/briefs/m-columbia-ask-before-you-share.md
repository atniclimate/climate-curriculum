# m-columbia-ask-before-you-share: Ask Before You Share

Cluster H. Status: concept brief, planning only (no build before D27).
Seeds: P2, L2.2, C-W4, C-A3, s12. Written from SEEDS.md as of commit 9c8d64f.

A rehearsal of consent, never a container for Knowledge. Learners practice
asking, listening, accepting "not now", and letting a speaker decide what
happens to each sentence, using only their own low-stakes material or invented
practice lines. Nothing an Elder, knowledge holder, or family member actually
said ever enters the page. Readiness per SEEDS: Decision (storage; D22 rename).

## 3. The interaction, sketched in words

One screen, three zones, used by a pair at one Chromebook or by a class on one
projector. A banner stays at the top: "Practice only. Do not type anything an
Elder, knowledge holder, or family member told you. This page forgets
everything when you close or reload it." Label: TEACHING MODEL.

1. Ask (checkpoint 1). The page shows the L2.2 consent script. The speaker
   presses one of: "Yes, notes are okay", "Yes, but no notes" (the round
   continues by listening only), or "Not now". "Not now" ends the round at
   once and shows: "Not now is a complete answer. Say thank you." No "why"
   prompt, no retry button, only "Switch roles".
2. Listen. The asker adds cards, one sentence each, either picked from the
   invented practice lines or typed (140 characters, see question 2). Practice
   lines are ordinary weather memories (a snow day, a smoky week, a hot night);
   none is attributed to any person, and none carries a place name in a
   language, a First Food, a teaching, or a Tribal reference.
3. Decide (checkpoint 2). The asker reads each card back. Only the speaker
   marks it: Share (goes on the class wall), Change (speaker rewords it), Hold
   (face down; stays with the speaker; decide later), Take back (gone from the
   page's memory). An unmarked card is treated as Hold and never reaches the
   wall. Any mark can be changed until the round ends, including taking a
   shared card back down.

The wall zone shows only Share cards, then "2 held with the speaker", and
nothing about taken-back cards. "End and clear" empties everything.
Enforcement: session memory only (no localStorage, no cookies), nothing
survives reload, no export, print, copy-all, or download control, no network
call. The page cannot stop someone typing real words; the banner, the
invented-lines default, the hand twin, and the teacher note carry that rule.
Storage is an open question tied to D23 (PENDING).

## 4. Hand twin

Index cards, a pen, chart paper labeled "Our wall", and four signals the
speaker can show without writing (four colored cards with the word printed,
or hand gestures: open hand Share, pencil motion Change, card to chest Hold,
card into own pocket Take back). Same three steps; the speaker keeps every
Held and Taken-back card and leaves with it, so nothing is thrown away in the
classroom. The observer's checklist is the half sheet. 15 minutes (6-12), 20
(CCIC 200), 10 (TCR 201), 3 (workshop: the facilitator holds up the four
signal cards). Learners may speak, write, point, or sign a mark.

## 7. Access

Keyboard: banner, Ask buttons, card list, then wall; each card's four marks
are one radio group (arrow keys move, Space selects); focus moves to the next
unmarked card after a mark; "Not now" and "End and clear" are always in the
tab order. No single-key shortcuts. Screen reader: each card is "Card 2 of 4:
<text>. Mark: Hold"; a polite live region says "Card 2 moved to the wall" or
"Card 2 taken back". Marks use word plus shape, never color alone; 44px
targets; visible focus. Reduced motion: cards appear and leave with no flip or
slide. Chromebook: one file, no data, no images required; estimated well under
100 KB and trivial at 4x CPU throttle (estimate, not measured). 390px: one
column, wall below the cards. Offline: runs from a downloaded folder with no
change.
