## 3. The interaction, sketched in words

One screen, two stacked panels sharing one time axis, a water year from 10/01 to 09/30.

- **Top panel (Frog's Eye, one station):** snow water equivalent at one SNOTEL station, drawn as a "bank balance" that fills through winter and drains in spring. A faint band shows the issuer's median for that date.
- **Bottom panel (Bird's Eye, one river):** daily flow at one USGS gauge for the same water year.
- **A "same water" bar** beside the panels: the station's total water-year precipitation for the year shown, next to its median. This is the check that makes "same water, different timing" true or false for this station (see section 9, row F1).
- **Controls:** two year buttons ("A typical year" and "2015"), a week scrubber, and a "Show as table" switch. Before the panels draw, the learner sets one prediction mark: "In which month does the bank run out?" The mark lives in session memory only.
- **What changes when they act:** switching the year redraws both panels; the scrubber moves a single vertical line through both, and the readout says the date, the snow water left, and the river flow that week. At the end of the scrub, the learner's prediction mark sits beside the real month the bank emptied.
- **Labels always visible:** "SNAPSHOT, captured [capture date], NRCS and USGS" on the panels; "TEACHING MODEL" on any tray or s04 reference. A Moon's Eye line under the panels says the 2015 low snow was West-wide, with its source once F1 and F2 close.
- **No salmon, harvest, or First Foods line.** Where L1.3 says timing matters for salmon, the page holds `[NEEDS TRIBAL CONFIRMATION: host Nation]` and nothing else.

## 4. Hand twin

- **The trays (L1.3 materials and procedure, 25 minutes of measuring inside the 50-minute lesson):** ice tray and rain tray with the same volume of water, cups read every five minutes, sponge added at 20 minutes. This already exists; the module does not change it.
- **Paper snapshot (5 minutes, no device, no internet):** the same two panels printed from the case pack, one sheet per pair, with a paper strip as the week marker slid along the time axis. Pairs color the snow panel where the bank is empty and circle the river's August flow in each year.
- **More than one way in:** hands (ice, water, the paper strip), eyes (cups and graphs), voice (a timekeeper or reader role for a learner who should not handle cold water), and writing (the annotation). A raised-line copy of the two curves (glue line on card stock) serves a learner who reads by touch; teacher prep about 10 minutes.

## 6. Place

- **Neutral form:** public geography and agency names only (a river, a gauge, a snow station, the Cascade crest, the Columbia). The page says plainly that this river runs through Indigenous homelands and names no Nation, story, or relationship.
- **What a Nation overlay would add, only with that Nation's recorded confirmation:** the Nation's own name for the river or the mountain, its observations of snow and seasons, and how snow timing touches its First Foods and harvest. - **Consent level: C1** (as SEEDS assigns): a courtesy notice to the host Nation's office when the module is used in that Nation's community, because the neutral data still sit in a Nation's homeland. Analytic roles that remain: a reader of the snow record, a reader of the river record, a water manager, a farmer.

## 7. Access

- **Keyboard path:** prediction mark (arrow keys move it by month, Enter sets it), then year buttons (aria-pressed), then the week scrubber (a native range input; arrow keys step a week, Page Up and Page Down a month), then "Show as table". Visible focus on each; no keyboard trap.
- **Screen-reader labels:** each panel is an SVG with role="img" and a title that names station, river, year, and the SNAPSHOT label; the scrubber's aria-valuetext reads "Week of [date]: snow water [n] inches; river [n] cubic feet per second", or "unknown" where no reading exists. The table view carries every plotted value.
- **Reduced motion:** no animation; the scrubber jumps, it does not glide. Nothing flashes.
- **Chromebook load estimate (not measured):** two daily series for two years plus medians, about 2,200 points, inline SVG, under 100 KB of data. Expected to stay smooth at 4x CPU throttle; the probe confirms before any merge.
- **390px:** panels stack, the "same water" bar drops below them, buttons go full width with 44px targets, and the readout sits under the scrubber.
