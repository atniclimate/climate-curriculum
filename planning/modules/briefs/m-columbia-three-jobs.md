# m-columbia-three-jobs: More Than One Job

Cluster I. Status: concept brief, planning only (no build before D27).
Module author: Patrick A. Freeland, with ATNI Climate. Licensing to be set later.
Seeds: P3, L3.2, T-M3, s15, s16. Written from SEEDS.md as of commit 835c6a1.

A classroom page and a paper set where learners stamp each action with every
job it does (Adaptation, Mitigation, Resilience), give a reason for each
stamp, and ask "who decides something like this?" It ships with a neutral
card set only. No card in the neutral set names a Nation, a Tribal project,
or a real place. The working title is a placeholder; D6 (the s16 title) is
PENDING and this brief does not choose it (see question 8).

## 3. The interaction, sketched in words

One screen. No data view, so no LIVE, SNAPSHOT, or TEACHING MODEL label is
needed; the page footer says "Example actions; no real place or project."

- Left: the card list (8 for grades 6-8, 12 for 9-12 and college). Each
  card shows its plain sentence and, once touched, the stamps it has earned.
- Center, the open card: three stamp buttons, A (circle), M (square), R
  (triangle), each a toggle, any combination. Pressing a stamp opens one
  short "because..." line under it. Unpressing removes the reason.
- Below the stamps: "Who decides something like this?" with chips for kinds
  of decider: me or my family; a school; a city or county; a Tribal
  government; a state or federal agency; a utility or business; a court;
  "the card does not say". More than one may be picked. Learners pick
  kinds; they never answer as any of them.
- One row, "Where does it act?": Frog's Eye (you and your family), Bird's
  Eye (community and watershed), Moon's Eye (region and planet).
- "Read about it" opens once a card has one stamp and one reason. The
  reading names the jobs most people see ("debate welcome"), the tool it
  needs, and one question. No "yes" or "look again".
- Bottom: a seven-part Venn that fills as cards are stamped. A card with A
  and R lands in that overlap. A card with no stamp sits outside, marked
  "not sorted yet", never "no job".
- A "More questions" switch (CCIC 200, TCR 201) adds two lines per card:
  one co-benefit, one way it could backfire.
- Last panel: pick one card and fill Who? How? When? Why? With whom?
- Session memory only; a "Clear my answers" button; nothing saved after
  reload (D23 default, PENDING).

## 4. Hand twin

The paper set is the floor; the page is optional.

- Flooding room (7 minutes, whole class, standing): the teacher mimes a tap
  running. One volunteer "turns it off" (Mitigation). Two lift chairs and
  books onto desks and mime mopping (Adaptation). The rest pass a "pantry"
  (any box) and a written plan hand to hand and help set the room back
  (Resilience). Learners who would rather not move call out which job each
  person is doing. A sketch of the room on the board serves a seated class.
- Stamp sort (18 minutes, teams of three or four): three yarn loops or hula
  hoops overlapped on the floor, or the same Venn printed on a desk sheet.
  Printed cards. Stamps are rubber stamps, stickers, or hand-drawn letters
  with the same shapes (circle A, square M, triangle R), so color is never
  the only cue. One sticky note per stamp: "because..." A second sticky note
  color (with a corner fold for touch) says who decides.
- Gallery check (10 minutes): teams visit another Venn and leave one
  question on a sticky note.
- Relational framing (4 minutes): a half-sheet with five boxes (Who? How?
  When? Why? With whom?) for one card; written, drawn, or said to a partner.
- More than one way in and out: cards can be placed, pointed to, or read
  aloud; reasons can be written, drawn, or dictated. Picture icons on the
  6-8 cards (L3.2 differentiation).
- No internet, no device, nothing collected. Sticky notes stay in the room.

## 7. Access

- Keyboard: Tab runs card list, stamps, reason lines, decider chips, scale
  row, reading, Venn summary, framing panel; arrow keys move in the card
  list; Space or Enter toggles a stamp; visible focus; 44px targets.
- Screen reader: stamps are toggle buttons with aria-pressed and names like
  "Mitigation stamp, square"; the Venn summary is also a plain list ("Rain
  garden: Adaptation"); the reading sits in a polite live region.
- Reduced motion: stamps appear without a press animation when
  prefers-reduced-motion is set; the Venn changes state, it does not slide.
- Chromebook load: one self-contained HTML file, inline SVG stamps and
  icons, no photos, estimated under 150 KB (not measured); 4x CPU throttle
  test at build.
- 390px: card list collapses to a scrolling strip above the open card; the
  Venn becomes the plain list with shape badges.
- Offline: no network calls; runs from a downloaded folder.
