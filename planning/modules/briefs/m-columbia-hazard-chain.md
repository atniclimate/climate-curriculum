## 3. The interaction, sketched in words

One screen, labeled "TEACHING MODEL: simplified chains. Real events are named only where a source is shown."

- **Left, the deck:** about 16 hazard cards in plain words, each with a small icon and no color-only meaning: heat wave, warm nights, warm winter, rain instead of snow, low snowpack, early melt, low summer flow, warm river, dry fuels, wildfire, smoke, burned bare slope, heavy rain, debris flow, sediment on the stream bed, cold-water fish under stress.
- **Right, the chain:** an empty vertical column. "Add to chain" puts a card at the bottom; "Move up", "Move down", and "Remove" sit on each placed card. No dragging is needed.
- **Between every two cards, a link** with the words "sets up" and three tag buttons: "from data", "from experience", "unknown". Every new link starts as "unknown": a hatched gap with the word "unknown". Choosing "from data" opens a short list of the case pack's dated sources (section 5); a link with no source chosen stays "unknown: no source named yet". "From experience" shows a dashed link with the words "someone here has seen this"; the page asks for no story, name, or place.
- **Three preset chains** (heat, snow drought, fire then rain; question 1), each link pre-tagged with its source or "unknown". Loading a preset replaces the column; "Start my own" clears it.
- **A count under the chain:** "6 links: 3 from data, 1 from experience, 2 unknown." No score, no "complete" badge. Removing an unknown link is allowed only by removing a card; the gap cannot be hidden.
- **End of every chain:** a closed box, "Who and what depends on this?", that the page never fills: `[NEEDS TRIBAL CONFIRMATION: host Nation]` for any relative, First Food, or community. Learners answer for themselves on paper.
- **Scale line:** Frog's Eye (one link you could see from your own yard), Bird's Eye (the whole chain in one watershed), Moon's Eye (which link is happening across the whole West in the same year).
- **Support line,** always visible below the chain: "Some of us have lived through these. You can pass, or pick a chain that did not touch your home." plus `[PLACEHOLDER: counseling and wellness contacts, filled by the host]`.

## 4. Hand twin

The primary route; the page is optional.

- **Card chain (20 minutes, pairs, no device):** one letter page of 16 hazard cards and a page of link strips. Each link strip has three faces: "from data" (solid border, a line to write the source and its date), "from experience" (dashed border), "unknown" (hatched, a "?" and a hole-punched corner so it can be found by touch). Pairs lay the chain on the desk and flip each strip to its tag. The teacher card holds the three preset chains with their sources.
- **Standing chain (8 minutes, whole class, 6-12 and workshop):** learners hold cards in a line, each with a hand on the next person's shoulder for a known link. An unknown link is a held-out hand that does not touch: the gap stays in the line, and the class says aloud who could close it.
- **More than one way in:** cards can be placed, pointed to, read aloud, or dictated; tags are shape, letter, and pattern, never color alone; a raised-line copy of the link strips (glue line on card stock, about 10 minutes of prep) serves a learner who reads by touch.
- No internet, no device, nothing collected; chains go home with learners or into the recycling.

## 6. Place

- **Neutral form:** public geography only: the Columbia Basin, the Cascades, a river, a forested slope. Real events (the 06/2021 heat, the 2015 warm river) appear in general terms with a dated primary source. No Nation, no reservation, no fishing place, no village is named or shown.
- **What a Nation overlay would add, only with that Nation's recorded confirmation:** that Nation's own account of how an event moved through its homelands (C2), and which relatives, First Foods, and people sit at the end of a chain, in what order, and how (C3). None of it is drafted: `[NEEDS TRIBAL CONFIRMATION: host Nation]`. C-W7 line 716 reports a Nation's fire-then-flood experience through the Robinson draft; the module does not carry it: `[NEEDS TRIBAL CONFIRMATION: Nez Perce Tribe]`.
- **Consent level: C0** for the neutral form, because nothing in it is Tribe-specific or Nation data; **C2** if a Nation's experience of an event becomes the case; **C3** for any First Food relationship.
- **Recent disasters:** the teacher card says do not run a chain about an event in a community it touched unless that Nation leads; carry the support line; "from experience" never asks for a story.
- **D22:** no roles. Learners are chain builders and readers of sources. No learner speaks as a Nation, council, Elder, fisher, or agency. L4.1's role cards stay in L4.1.

## 7. Access

- **Keyboard path:** deck (a list; arrow keys move, Enter adds to the chain), then the chain (each card's Move up, Move down, Remove buttons), then each link's tag group (a radio group: arrow keys choose, the source list opens as a native select), then presets and "Start my own". Visible focus on every control; no trap.
- **Screen-reader labels:** each link reads, for example, "Link 3, dry fuels sets up wildfire: unknown, no source named yet"; the count line is a polite live region; the closed end box reads "Who and what depends on this: not filled by this page."
- **Reduced motion:** nothing animates; cards appear in place.
- **Chromebook load estimate (not measured):** text, inline SVG icons, and four fact lines; under 60 KB, no data series. Expected smooth at 4x CPU throttle; the probe confirms before any merge.
- **390px:** the deck collapses to a select above the chain; tag buttons stack at full width with 44px targets.
