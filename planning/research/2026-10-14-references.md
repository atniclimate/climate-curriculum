# S4 References Synthesis: VERIFY, MEDIA-CREDITS, corrections, resources, quotes

Prepared 10/14/2026 by the references synthesizer (S4) for the Columbia River Climate Change
Teachers curriculum (repo `atniclimate/climate-curriculum`, branch `dev/revisions-2026-10`).
Nothing here has been applied to the repo. Everything is a proposal for Patrick and Cayla to rule on.

## How this was built

- Merged from nine gather reports (D1 to D9), each written and source-checked on 10/14/2026.
  D1 covered clouds and weather, D2 salmon, rivers and dams, D3 species and photos, D4 education
  frameworks, D5 media literacy and quotes, D6 the CCIC-200 syllabus, D7 the TCR-201 syllabus,
  D8 the lessons, guiding document, README and the remaining template facts, and D9 online
  education materials.
- The synthesizer read all nine reports in full and did not re-fetch any web source. Every
  "checked" date below is the gather agent's date (10/14/2026). The synthesizer did re-read the
  quoted repo lines (template.html, the three curriculum files, the guiding document,
  VERIFY.md, MEDIA-CREDITS.md) to confirm the locators and current wording.
- Public sources only. Private Drive files, private archives and anything in the git-ignored
  `library/` folder are not cited. Where a finding rested on a private item, it is left out or
  turned into a question for Patrick.
- Status rule used in Section 1: **CHECKED** means confirmed as written. **OPEN** means not
  confirmed, or confirmed but the text needs a change that has not been made yet ("fix proposed"
  in Notes). **CHANGED** is held back until the slide text is actually edited. That keeps the
  file honest: no row says CHANGED for text that is still live.
- "Search snippet" means the gather agent saw the wording only in search results, not on the
  fetched page. Those items are flagged and kept OPEN unless a second fetched source confirms them.
